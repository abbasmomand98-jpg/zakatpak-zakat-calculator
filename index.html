Who can receive your Zakat?

Zakat may only go to eight groups, named in the Quran (9:60). Every payment in ZakatPak names the group it reaches — so the Zakat is valid, and you can prove where it went.

RED-FLAG SPOTTER

Spot the fake collector.

Four things a collector does that should stop you paying — and one safe habit. A collector asking for cash with no receipt; a "charity" with no registration number and no office, WhatsApp only; a promise that your Zakat will reach orphans abroad with no named partner and no accounts; and pressure — "pay now, the truck leaves tonight". Call each one a red flag or safe, then check your score.
